OCCO Dinner Club is a Lebanese restaurant at Kalendegatan 12 in Malmö. The table shares mezze and grill dishes, and during dinner dancers, magicians and live musicians take the room. Open Tuesday to Saturday from 17:00.

Tue–Thu 17:00–23:00 Fri–Sat 17:00–00:00 Kalendegatan 12, 211 35 Malmö See the menu

Mezze for the whole table

Order a few dishes each and pass them round. Many mezze are vegetarian or vegan, and both set menus can be made either way. With it, wine from Lebanon and twelve house cocktails.
